    I'm Theo, an independent product and brand designer in Ghent. For eleven years I've helped banks, logistics teams and small brands turn complicated things into tools that feel obvious.

    Background

    I started in print, moved to interaction design, and spent four years leading the product team at Fieldnote Studio in Antwerp. Since 2021 I've worked on my own, partnering directly with founders and in-house teams.

    My work sits between research and visual craft. I like to sit with the people who'll use a product, sketch fast, prototype early, and keep the design system tidy enough that engineers enjoy using it.

    Outside client work I teach a monthly interface design workshop and ride gravel around the Flemish Ardennes.
